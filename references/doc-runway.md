# Template: RUNWAY & FUNDRAISE

The company's money, kept strictly separate from any customer or user funds you hold. The burn, the runway target as a formula, and the raise as structure. Tier 3.

## Questions it must answer

- What are the two pools that must NEVER be conflated (company capital vs funds you hold for others)?
- What's the burn model - structure, not just a total?
- What's the runway target, as a formula and not a fixed month-count?
- What's the raise - structure and considerations, before any amount?
- What are the sensitivities and risks?
- How does it map to milestones?

## Required sections

- `## Two pools, never conflated` - company money vs custodied / customer money. State the hard wall between them.
- `## The burn model` - the cost structure, parameterized.
- `## Runway target (a formula, not a month-count)`.
- `## The raise` - stage, instrument, use-of-funds, considerations. No amount until the model produces one.
- `## Sensitivities & risks`.
- `## Milestone mapping`.

## Good vs slop

Good: company burn and held funds are walled apart, and runway is a formula you can re-run as inputs change. Slop: one bank balance, one number, and a fundraise amount picked from vibes.
