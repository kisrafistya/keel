# Template: GO-TO-MARKET (design partners, onboarding, success)

How the first customers come in, get live, and succeed. The motion, the agreement, onboarding, support, and the customer's own economic case. Tier 3.

## Questions it must answer

- What's the customer-acquisition motion for the first cohort?
- What's the design-partner / LOI scope?
- How does onboarding actually work, step by step?
- What does support and success look like operationally?
- What's the economic framing FOR the customer (their case to say yes)?
- How does this map to milestones?
- What's still open?

## Required sections

- `## Purpose`.
- `## The motion` - how the first cohort is found and closed.
- `## Partner agreement / LOI scope` - what both sides commit to.
- `## Onboarding motion` - the steps from yes to live.
- `## Support & success ops`.
- `## The economic framing for the customer` - why it pays for THEM, in their numbers.
- `## Milestone mapping`.
- `## Opens` - flagged, not papered over.

## Good vs slop

Good: the customer's reason to say yes is stated in their economics, not yours. Slop: "we'll do content and outreach" with no motion and no LOI scope.
