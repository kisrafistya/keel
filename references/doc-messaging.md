# Template: MESSAGING canon

The words. The locked tagline, one-liner, the problem statement, the spoken answer, and - critically - what NOT to say. Tier 3. Keep it short; this is a canon, not an essay.

## Questions it must answer

- What's the tagline (a few words)?
- What's the one-liner / headline version of the wedge?
- What's the problem, in one locked sentence?
- What's the mission?
- What's the elevator / investor paragraph?
- How do you answer "what are you building?" out loud (spoken, not deck-prose)?
- What are the honest caveats, so you don't overclaim?
- What must you NOT say?

## Required sections

- `## Tagline` - a few words, locked.
- `## One-liner` - the wedge as a headline.
- `## The problem (one sentence, locked)`.
- `## The mission`.
- `## The paragraph` - the elevator / investor answer.
- `## The category (what we are, and are not)`.
- `## Conversational ("what are you building?")` - the spoken version.
- `## Supporting one-liners` - pull as needed.
- `## Honest caveats` - what you will not overclaim.
- `## What NOT to say` - the phrasings that misrepresent or trigger the wrong category. This section is mandatory.

## Good vs slop

Good: two people on the team describe the product the same way, and both avoid the same landmines. Slop: a tagline plus a paragraph of adjectives, and no "what not to say" - so everyone improvises and the story drifts. Preserve phrasing that already works; state a reason before replacing a line that lands.
