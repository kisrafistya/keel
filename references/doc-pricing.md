# Template: PRICING & unit economics

What you charge on, why, and whether it pays. The metric, the cost map, the formula, the tiers, the break-evens, and the parameters you must MEASURE before fixing. Tier 3.

## Questions it must answer

- What's the billing metric, and why that one and not the obvious default?
- What drives cost, and what's the price lever per driver?
- What's the pricing formula and the tiers?
- What's the key spread / margin, and how sensitive is it?
- What's the cost-to-serve, including any free tier?
- Where are the break-evens?
- Which parameters are still guesses that must be measured?

## Required sections

- `## The metric` - what you charge on, and why it beats the default (e.g. usage vs seats).
- `## Activity & cost map` - every cost-driving action and its cost.
- `## One price lever per cost driver`.
- `## The pricing formula`.
- `## The tiers`.
- `## The key spread / margin` with `### sensitivity` (one page).
- `## Cost-to-serve & free-tier cap`.
- `## Unit economics` - the break-evens, kept distinct.
- `## Open parameters (MEASURE before fixing)`.

## Good vs slop

Good: every constant is either sourced or flagged "measure before fixing," and the free tier has a sponsorship cap. Slop: three tiers with round numbers and no cost model behind them. Never invent a conversion rate or a margin - that violates the honest-content discipline.
