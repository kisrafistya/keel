# Pressure-test: idea vs insight

Use before committing to a build, or on demand via `keel pressure-test`. The job is to find whether there is a real, non-consensus **insight** - not to grade an idea against consensus. An adversarial pass run on consensus data is structurally biased to kill a true insight, because from the outside a real insight is indistinguishable from being wrong. Run the honest test instead.

## Step 1 - separate the idea from the insight

- **The idea** is the consensus version: what anyone would say it is. If that is the whole pitch, well-funded players are already there and you are late.
- **The insight** is the non-consensus belief underneath, usually born of lived experience: the thing you believe that the market prices wrong. State it in one sentence.
- If you can only find the idea and not the insight, **say so plainly.** That is the finding, not a failure.

## Step 2 - the three questions

- **Why now** - what changed that makes this possible or urgent today, and not three years ago.
- **Why you** - the unfair, hard-to-replicate edge. Be specific. "We will work harder" is not an edge.
- **Why different** - and test it against the **best-positioned incumbent, not the current market.** The real question is "why won't the giant who already has the distribution, the licence, the users, just do this?"

## Step 3 - the leap and the breaks

- State the one or two **leap-of-faith assumptions** the whole thing rests on, crisply.
- For each, name the **cheap, falsifiable test that would prove it WRONG.** "What would make me wrong" is the test. "What confirms me" is not.

## Step 4 - the calibrated verdict

Keep three things distinct and don't blur them:

- A genuine non-consensus insight (strong: lived, falsifiable, load-bearing).
- A consensus idea wearing an insight's clothes (you're late; the components are commodities).
- A real leap-of-faith vs a weak point - calibrate by **evidence type**: structural/logical reasoning is stronger evidence than a consensus assertion.

Do not kill a non-consensus insight from consensus. Do not bless a consensus idea as an insight. End on the single load-bearing belief and what would break it, not a thumbs-up.

## Output format

1. **The idea** - one line.
2. **The insight** - one line, or "no insight found, only the idea."
3. **Why-now / why-you / why-different** - one line each; why-different must name the incumbent it survives.
4. **Leap-of-faith assumption(s).**
5. **What would break each** - the cheap, falsifiable test.
6. **Verdict** - the load-bearing belief, where it's strong, where it's fragile.
